# External Guide: Program Manager

## Your Role in the Pilot
As a Program Manager, your primary interest is **portfolio visibility** and **decision lineage**. You use the framework to understand which projects are moving forward, what evidence supports them, and how team capacity is being allocated.

## Key Artifacts to Care About
- **Executive Brief:** High-level summary of program status and key prioritizations.
- **Operating Review Packet:** Detailed look at workflow health and decision consistency across cycles.
- **Capacity Retrospective:** Understanding how team effort maps to structural review complexity.

## What Success Looks Like
- You have an auditable trail of *how* and *when* prioritizations were made.
- Program bottlenecks in the review process are identified and addressed.
- The team's structural review capacity is clearly understood.

## What to Ignore
- You can ignore individual residue-level contact tables and complex structural sensitivity plots.
- Focus on the high-level summary reports and briefs.

## Caveats
- Portfolio prioritization supports decisions under limited capacity; it does not claim optimal scientific truth.
- Success metrics are based on workflow adoption and decision consistency.
