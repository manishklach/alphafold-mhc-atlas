# External Guide: Scientist

## Your Role in the Pilot
As a Scientist using this framework, your primary goal is to perform **interpretable structural reviews** of peptide–MHC perturbations. You will evaluate structural evidence to prioritize mutations for experimental follow-up.

## Key Artifacts to Care About
- **Review Packet:** Your primary workspace for inspecting individual variants and providing human judgment.
- **Decision Packet:** The summary of a review cycle, containing consensus and shortlists.
- **Hypothesis Table:** Transparent, structure-linked hypotheses generated from your reviews.

## What Success Looks Like
- You can clearly explain *why* a variant was prioritized based on structural contact changes.
- Your review decisions are tracked and auditable.
- You generate actionable hypotheses for wet-lab validation.

## What to Ignore
- You can likely ignore organizational dashboards and capacity planning tools.
- Focus on the project review workflow.

## Caveats
- This is **not** a binding affinity predictor.
- All structural insights are exploratory and require experimental validation.
