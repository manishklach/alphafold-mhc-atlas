# Pilot Onboarding: Manager / Reviewer

Welcome to the structural decision platform evaluation.

## Your Role in the Pilot
Your main goal is to evaluate if this platform improves the speed and clarity of decision-making, reduces "churn" (repeatedly discussing the same unresolved questions), and ensures follow-ups are tracked.

## What to Do First
1. **Read the Evaluation Guide:** Look at `outputs/evaluation_pack/PILOT_EVALUATION_GUIDE.md`.
2. **Use Your Role View:** Open the app and navigate to `Role Views` -> `Manager`. This hides the raw structural data and highlights decisions.
3. **Review the Retrospective:** Look at `Retrospective Reports` to see cycle-over-cycle program velocity.

## What to Trust vs Ignore
- **Trust:** The decision lineage. If an item is marked "stalled," it is genuinely stalled across multiple weeks.
- **Ignore:** Certainty. Manager-facing outputs intentionally preserve caveats. 

## Common Mistakes
- **Treating task completion as scientific validation:** Just because an execution task is "completed" does not mean the underlying structural hypothesis was biologically validated.
