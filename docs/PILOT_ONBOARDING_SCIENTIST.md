# Pilot Onboarding: Scientist

Welcome to the structural decision platform evaluation.

## Your Role in the Pilot
Your main goal is to evaluate if this platform makes structure-guided mutation review easier, more reproducible, and clearer during team discussions. 

## What to Do First
1. **Explore the Golden Demo:** Start the app (`mhc-atlas app --demo golden_weekly_review_demo`) and go to `Variant Explorer`.
2. **Review Shortlists:** Navigate to `Review Queues / Shortlists` and inspect the structural evidence driving the prioritization.
3. **Log Annotations:** Use the `Notes` and `Feedback` tabs to capture caveats.

## What to Trust vs Ignore
- **Trust:** The explicit structural contacts, pocket deltas, and mutation evidence.
- **Ignore:** Any assumption that ranking = biological truth. This is an exploratory tool for hypothesis generation.

## Common Mistakes
- **Assuming direct binding affinity:** The platform does *not* predict binding affinity or immunogenicity.
- **Skipping caveats:** Always add biological caveats (e.g., "solvent exposed, likely flexible") to your review decisions.
