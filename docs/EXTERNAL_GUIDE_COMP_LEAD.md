# External Guide: Computational Lead

## Your Role in the Pilot
As a Computational Lead, you are responsible for the **analytical configuration** and **robustness validation** of the framework for your team. You ensure the structural thresholds and prioritization weights align with your program's scientific goals.

## Key Artifacts to Care About
- **Project Configuration:** Where you define alleles, reference peptides, and ranking modes.
- **Robustness Report:** Analysis of how stable your prioritizations are under varying assumptions.
- **Benchmarking Results:** Comparison of structural evidence against known external binders or disruptors.

## What Success Looks Like
- The team has a stable, repeatable workflow for mutation review.
- Analytical assumptions are clearly documented and tested via sensitivity analysis.
- The framework surface is correctly narrowed for your scientists.

## What to Ignore
- You can ignore the low-level UI styling and focus on the command-line outputs and configuration logic.

## Caveats
- Sensitivity testing clarifies assumptions; it does not create model "truth."
- Ensure the team remains aware of the "What It Does Not Claim" guardrails.
