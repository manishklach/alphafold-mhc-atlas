# Golden Demo Walkthrough

## Audience

- scientist evaluating ranked perturbations
- computational lead reviewing prioritization logic
- manager or collaborator reviewing what needs a decision this week

## 10-minute walkthrough

1. Launch the app on the golden workspace.
2. Open `Workspace Overview` and confirm the portfolio and available artifacts.
3. Open `Changes Since Last Review` and note what actually changed.
4. Open `Weekly Review Packet` and generate the packet if needed.
5. Open `Role Views` and compare scientist vs manager emphasis.
6. Open `Open Questions` and `Next Actions`.
7. Open `Review Queues / Shortlists` to see what is already being carried into discussion.
8. Open the manager decision packet and review the caveats.

## What to say in a demo

- The product is not trying to replace scientific judgment.
- It is trying to make recurring structure-guided review workflows more reproducible and less ad hoc.
- Every recommendation is still linked back to concrete evidence tables and review state.
- Manager-facing outputs summarize decisions, but do not hide caveats.

## Suggested close

End on `Next Actions` and the manager decision packet. That best communicates the wedge from analysis to team decision workflow.
